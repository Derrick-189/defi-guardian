About DeFi Guardian

Our mission to secure the future of decentralized finance

Our Mission

DeFi Guardian was born from a simple observation: billions of dollars in DeFi were being lost to preventable smart contract vulnerabilities. Traditional testing simply cannot guarantee the absence of bugs in immutable code.

Our mission is to make formal verification accessible to every smart contract developer. By combining multiple verification tools — SPIN model checking, Coq and Lean theorem provers, and Rust verifiers — we provide mathematical guarantees of correctness that go far beyond conventional testing.

Our Team

Developed at Kabale University's Department of Computer Science, DeFi Guardian represents cutting-edge research in applied formal methods for blockchain security.

Key Achievements

  • Integration of 6 verification tools
  • 3D state space visualization
  • Cross-language support (Solidity + Rust)
  • Monte Carlo risk simulation
  • Multi-tool triangulation
